# Parshes left unbuilt

A parsha stays unbuilt when its source book has nothing that retells its chapters. Nothing is filled in from
another book.

## Shmoys … Dvorim — *Khumesh far kinder loyt Yehoash* (1940, `nybc210870`)

Sub-sections were assigned to the parsha whose verses they abridge (each sentence matched to its closest
Yehoyesh verse; a sub-section goes to the parsha with the largest share of its text). Several sub-sections in
chapter יא ("די קינדער פֿון ישׂראל קריגן געזעצן", pp. 177–197) are thematic collages of laws from up to five
parshes; those were assigned by majority and the close calls are listed in the agent report.

| parsha | why unbuilt |
|---|---|
| terumah | The book has no sub-section on the command to build the Mishkan (Ex. 25–27); it retells only the building itself ("דער משכּן", Ex. 35–36, assigned to Vayakhel). |
| tetzaveh | Nothing on the priestly garments, the oil or the consecration (Ex. 27:20–30:10). |
| pekudei | The only Pekudei material is the last two sentences of "דער משכּן" (Ex. 39:32, 40:34), which is mostly Vayakhel; a sub-section is not split. |
